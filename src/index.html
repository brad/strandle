How to play

Strandle mixes Wordle's colored guesses with Strands' themed letter web.

  1. Theme words fill the 6×6 grid. One is the spangram: it spans opposite sides and names the theme.
  2. Use up to six 4–8-letter probes, or start tracing early for leftover-probe bonus points.
  3. Drag adjacent letters, including diagonals. Trace letters in word order; palindromes may be submitted in reverse.
  4. After tracing begins, incorrect paths build progress toward a hint.
